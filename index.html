My Journey

My Software Development Journey

Hello, I’m Azeem — a passionate Software Developer who loves turning ideas into real digital solutions. My journey started with a simple “Hello World” program, and since then, I’ve been driven by the vision of using technology to solve real-world problems in my community. I specialize in both frontend and backend development, working with technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, PHP, and Python. Over time, I’ve built and contributed to several projects, including the AfriWed Rwanda wedding media platform, the AzBeta online live studio, and the Healthy Coffee Plant Health Intelligence , which is actively used today. I am a self-learner who continuously improves through practice, online learning, and real project experience. Currently, I am focused on strengthening my backend and API development skills while working on impactful systems like healthcare and booking platforms. My goal is to grow into a professional Software Engineer and contribute to meaningful, large-scale solutions that make a difference.
